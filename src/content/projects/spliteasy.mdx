## Overview

SplitEasy AI turns a receipt image or PDF—and the way a group naturally describes who had what—into
a split that can be checked before anyone pays. It is designed around a simple constraint: AI can
accelerate the tedious parts, but it should never silently decide the final numbers.

The result is a review-first workflow rather than a one-shot chatbot response.

## Product flow

1. Upload a receipt PDF or image and run OCR.
2. Review the normalized receipt table, including quantities, discounts, deposits, and the printed
   total.
3. Add people and describe the split with text, a chat screenshot, or a voice transcript.
4. Review the proposed assignments and choose equal, per-item, weighted, percentage, or fixed-amount
   allocation where needed.
5. Export a Revolut-friendly summary, a detailed breakdown, CSV, or JSON.

Line items can retain evidence references back to OCR text and receipt regions. That makes an odd
price or quantity inspectable instead of asking the user to trust a model-generated table.

## Reliability before automation

Receipt OCR is noisy, and plausible-looking arithmetic is still wrong arithmetic. SplitEasy runs AI
output through deterministic parsing and reconciliation before it reaches the settlement view.
Money is calculated in integer minor units, currencies with zero decimal places such as HUF are
handled explicitly, and receipt totals are checked against the sum of active items.

The parser distinguishes product rows from quantity details, payment metadata, discounts, deposit
fees, and receipt-level totals. AI assignment proposals remain separate from manual assignments
until the user applies them. Multi-currency splits require an explicit settlement currency and
manual exchange rates rather than silently combining incompatible totals.

## Architecture and privacy

The interface is a static React application. Same-origin Cloudflare Pages Functions own OCR,
structuring, assignment, one-click splitting, model health, and transcription calls. Provider keys
for Mistral, OpenAI, and ElevenLabs stay server-side and never enter the browser bundle, local
storage, or exported split data.

## Current state

SplitEasy is an actively developed private prototype, not a public production service. The core
receipt-review, assignment, reconciliation, evidence, multi-currency, local-history, and export
flows are implemented; current work is focused on hardening real-world receipt parsing and reducing
the amount of manual repair needed after OCR.
