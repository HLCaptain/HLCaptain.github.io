LexiDash Arena is a compact race UI where text entry, progress feedback, and mobile layout all have
to stay legible under pressure.

## Focus

The interesting work is in the surface area between interaction and performance: a word grid that
fits narrow screens, progress indicators that do not thrash rendering, and online-room primitives
that can evolve without making local play fragile.
